Privacy policy

Effective: 27.09.2026

Zero cookies, zero ads, zero analytics. Here is the full, honest list of what still happens to your data.

This policy describes what actually happens to your data when you visit SEKTOR_X. In short: I don't collect personal data, I don't use cookies, ads or analytics, and the site loads nothing from third-party servers (the only exception is DNS lookups made by the domain scanner when you use it — see below). The details are below, because “no fluff” applies here too.

1. Who runs the site

The controller of SEKTOR_X (sec-stack.github.io/sektor-x) is its author. Contact: reply to any newsletter email, or open an issue on GitHub.

2. Hosting — GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub processes technical connection data, including your IP address, for example for security purposes. I have no access to those logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

3. Fonts

All fonts are hosted together with the site. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other external server to display a page.

4. Browser storage (localStorage)

If you switch the site language (PL/EN), your choice is stored locally in your browser under the key sx_lang. It never leaves your device and you can remove it by clearing site data. It is not a cookie and is not used for tracking.

5. Tools: password generator and quiz

The password generator, strength calculator and quiz run entirely in your browser. Generated passwords and answers are never sent or stored anywhere.

5a. Domain security scanner

The scanner runs in your browser. To check a domain, your browser sends its name to public DNS resolvers: Cloudflare (cloudflare-dns.com) and, as a fallback, Google (dns.google). They see your IP address and the domain being checked, under their own privacy policies. The scanner also connects to the scanned domain's website to verify HTTPS. I don't store scanned domains or results.

6. Newsletter — Substack

The newsletter runs on Substack. When you subscribe, you give your email address directly to Substack, which processes it under its own privacy policy. I can see the subscriber list and use it only to send the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.

6a. E-books and tools — Naffy and Gumroad

E-books and tools are delivered through Naffy and Gumroad. Any email or payment details you enter there are processed by those services (and their payment providers) under their own policies; I see the data needed to fulfil the order, such as the buyer's or downloader's email address.

7. External links

Articles link to sources and tools on other sites (e.g. haveibeenpwned.com). Once you click, that site's privacy policy applies.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing and to object. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl), or with the authority in your own EU country.

9. Changes

If something new appears on the site — for example cookie-free analytics or affiliate links — I will update this policy and the date at the top. The Polish version of this policy is the reference version.
