First Entry — Why I'm doing this at all

Everyone who builds anything online says to "document the journey". For a long time I thought it was an empty cliché. Now I understand what it actually means.

It's not about showing how smart you are. It's about being able to come back a year from now and see where you started. And about someone at the beginning of their own path knowing they're not alone.

Operator Base is my unfiltered space. Security, IT, productivity, whatever I'm currently experimenting with — I write about what interests me. If it's useful to someone else, great.
